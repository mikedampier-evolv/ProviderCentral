# Provider 360 — Persona Demo Scripts

Four role-based demo walkthroughs for the Provider 360 platform. Each script is written from the perspective of the persona, highlighting the features and insights most relevant to their daily work.

---

## Script 1: The Analyst

**Role:** H360_ANALYST | **Duration:** ~5 minutes  
**Focus:** Self-service analytics, Cortex Analyst (text-to-SQL), data exploration, semantic views

---

### Opening

> "As a data analyst supporting our health system, I spend my time answering ad-hoc questions from clinical and operational leaders. The problem with traditional BI is that every new question means a ticket, a queue, and days of turnaround. Let me show you how Provider 360 changes that."

### Scene 1: Provider Dashboard — Understanding the Landscape

1. **Open the Provider Dashboard page**
   - "This is my starting point. Six KPIs give me immediate orientation: 200K+ encounters, 12% readmission rate, 15.2% leakage, 84.3% OR utilization, $M in denied charges, and over 1,200 ML predictions running natively."
   - "The monthly trend sparklines tell me immediately whether things are getting better or worse — I don't need to write a query to answer 'is readmission rate improving?'"

2. **Click into Readmission & LOS**
   - "If a CMO asks me 'which DRGs are driving our readmission penalty,' I can answer in seconds."
   - Apply the facility filter and encounter type filter
   - "The Top 10 DRGs by readmission rate are ranked visually. I can see immediately that sepsis and heart failure are our biggest pain points."
   - Expand the ML Drivers section: "Cortex ML's TOP_INSIGHTS function automatically decomposed our readmission rate into contributing segments — DRG, payer, age bucket, department. No Python notebook, no data science request needed."

### Scene 2: Cortex Analyst — Conversational Analytics

3. **Open the Chat Widget (Cortex Agent)**
   - "But what about questions that don't map to a pre-built dashboard? Here's where it gets powerful."

   What is the readmission rate by facility and payer type?

   - "The semantic view behind this chat understands our data model — 6 tables, 18 metrics, 12 verified queries. It generates the correct SQL, executes it, and returns results."
   - "I didn't write SQL. I didn't file a ticket. I just asked."

4. **Ask a cross-domain question:**

   Which specialties have the highest referral leakage rate and what is their average lost revenue per referral?

   - "This spans our leakage mart. The semantic layer handles the join logic — I just describe what I want."

5. **Ask a predictive question:**

   Show me the encounter volume forecast for the next 90 days by facility?

   - "We're now querying ML prediction tables through natural language. The forecast was generated by Cortex ML's FORECAST function — trained and scored entirely inside Snowflake."

### Scene 3: Predictive Analytics — ML Without a Data Science Team

6. **Navigate to Predictive Analytics page**
   - "These four models — encounter forecast, denial anomaly detection, readmission drivers, and leakage drivers — run on a daily schedule via Snowflake Tasks."
   - Point to anomaly detection chart: "The model flagged 4 anomalous periods in denial volume. I can drill into those dates and correlate with operational events — was there a payer policy change? A coding update?"
   - "The key differentiator: I'm using ML outputs without managing infrastructure. No SageMaker, no model registry, no deployment pipeline."

### Closing

> "As an analyst, this platform gives me three things I've never had simultaneously: self-service dashboards for known questions, conversational AI for ad-hoc exploration, and embedded ML for predictive insights — all governed by the same security model, all running in one place."

## Script 2: The Clinician

**Role:** H360_CLINICIAN | **Duration:** ~5 minutes  
**Focus:** Patient-level data, PHI access, readmission risk, referral patterns, quality metrics

---

### Opening

> "As a hospitalist and medical director, I need to see actual patient data — names, MRNs, diagnoses — to make clinical decisions. I also need to understand which of my patients are at highest readmission risk and where our referral network is leaking. Let me show you what that looks like."

### Scene 1: Patient Records — Full PHI Access

1. **Open the Patient Records page**
   - "Notice the role in the top-right: I'm logged in as a Clinician."
   - Point to the masking policy table: "As a clinician, I see full patient names, full dates of birth, the last 4 of SSN, complete MRNs, full phone numbers and email."
   - "If I switch to the Executive role..." — toggle role dropdown — "...the same query returns pseudonymized names, year-only DOB, and fully masked SSN. Same table, same SQL. Snowflake's dynamic masking policies enforce this at the engine level."
   - Switch back to Clinician: "This is critical for my workflow. I can't make clinical decisions about 'PATIENT_a3f2c8b1.' I need to know it's John Smith, age 72, with a history of CHF."

### Scene 2: Readmission & Length of Stay — Clinical Quality

2. **Navigate to Readmission & LOS page**
   - "30-day readmissions are a CMS penalty metric. Our system-wide rate is 12%. But averages hide the real story."
   - Filter to Inpatient only, then filter to a specific facility

Show me 30 day readmissions by Inpatient only
Show me 30 day readmissions by Facility

   - "Now I can see which DRGs at my facility are driving readmissions. Sepsis, Heart Failure, Pneumonia — these are my targets for discharge planning interventions."
   - Point to the LOS Index: "Patients with an LOS index above 1.5 are staying 50% longer than expected for their DRG. That's either a complication, a social determinant issue, or a discharge planning failure."

3. **Expand the ML Drivers section:**
   - "Cortex ML identified that Medicare patients over 75 with sepsis have a 28% readmission rate — nearly triple our average. That's a cohort I can build a care pathway around."
   - "I didn't ask a data team to run this analysis. The model refreshes daily and surfaces new insights automatically."

### Scene 3: Patient Referral Leakage — Network Integrity

4. **Navigate to Patient Leakage page**
   - "Referral leakage means patients I refer to a specialist end up going outside our network. That's lost revenue, but more importantly, it's a continuity of care problem."
   - "15.2% leakage rate across 48K referrals. Which specialties are leaking most?"
   - Point to the specialty breakdown: "Orthopedics and Cardiology have the highest dollar leakage. If I'm referring patients to ortho and they're leaving our system, I need to understand why."
   - "Is it access? Wait times? Patient preference? Geography? This data helps me have that conversation with my specialty colleagues."

### Scene 4: Staffing & Quality — The Clinical-Operational Connection

5. **Navigate to Staffing & Quality page**
   - "Here's something that connects my clinical world to operations: the relationship between nurse staffing levels and readmission rates."
   - Point to the scatter plot: "Departments with lower hours-per-patient tend to have higher readmission rates. The data shows understaffed units have a readmission rate of X% versus Y% for adequately staffed units."
   - "This gives me evidence when I advocate for staffing — it's not just a nursing opinion, it's data-driven quality correlation."

### Closing

> "As a clinician, I need full patient context to make decisions, evidence to target interventions, and data to advocate for resources. This platform gives me all three without waiting for a report or filing an analytics ticket — and the governance model ensures I only see what's appropriate for my role."

## Script 4: The Finance Leader

**Role:** H360_FINANCE | **Duration:** ~5 minutes  
**Focus:** Revenue cycle, denials, cost structure, operating margin, financial forecasting

---

### Opening

> "As the VP of Revenue Cycle, denied claims are my number one priority. Every denied line is revenue we earned but can't collect. I also need to understand our cost structure, margin trends, and where cash recovery opportunities exist. Let me walk through how this platform surfaces those insights."

### Scene 1: Denials & Revenue Cycle — The Revenue Leak

1. **Navigate to Denials & Revenue Cycle page**
   - "116K denied claim lines. Let's understand where the money is."
   - Point to the denial category breakdown:
     - "Eligibility denials — patient wasn't covered at time of service. Often a front-desk registration issue."
     - "Prior Authorization — service wasn't pre-approved. This is a workflow problem."
     - "Coding errors — documentation didn't support the code billed. Fixable with CDI programs."
     - "Medical Necessity — payer disagrees the service was needed. Hardest to overturn."
   - "Each category has different root causes and different recovery strategies."

2. **Point to Appeal Success Rates:**
   - "Coding errors have a 65% appeal success rate — that's our highest ROI for rework effort. Medical necessity is only 35%. We should prioritize coding denial appeals."
   - "Timely filing compliance by payer shows us which payers have the tightest windows. If we're missing filing deadlines, that's pure preventable loss."

3. **Show the Anomaly Detection overlay:**
   - "Cortex ML flagged 4 anomalous periods in our denial volume. Let me zoom into the most recent spike."
   - "This spike in eligibility denials correlates with our implementation of a new payer contract. The system caught it automatically — we didn't wait for month-end reporting."
   - "Anomaly detection runs daily as part of our Snowflake Task DAG. By the time my team sits down Monday morning, any unusual pattern from the weekend is already flagged."

### Scene 2: Financial Performance — Margin Management

4. **Navigate to Financial Performance page**
   - Point to the KPIs: "Total revenue $XM, total expense $YM, operating margin Z%."
   - "Cost per CMI-adjusted discharge normalizes for acuity — so I'm comparing apples to apples across facilities with different patient populations."
   - Point to the cost breakdown chart: "Labor is our biggest cost driver at X% of total expense. Supplies are second. This monthly stacked view shows me when labor costs are creeping."

5. **Point to Department-level cost:**
   - "Cost per CMI discharge by department tells me which service lines are efficient and which need intervention."
   - "I can see that cardiac surgery has the highest cost but also the highest margin. General medicine has moderate cost but thin margins — that's where volume leverage matters."

6. **Point to Facility Margin comparison:**
   - "Facility A is at 12% margin, Facility E is at 4%. I now have a data-driven conversation with that facility's leadership about operational efficiency."

### Scene 3: AI-Powered Revenue Recovery

7. **Open the Chat Widget**
   - Type: **"What is our total denied revenue by category, and which category has the highest appeal success rate?"**
   - "Instead of running multiple reports, I ask one question and get a synthesized answer."
   - Type: **"Which facilities have the highest denial rates for prior authorization, and what is the estimated recoverable revenue?"**
   - "The agent cross-references denial data with facility data and appeal success rates to estimate recovery opportunity. That's a multi-step analysis done in seconds."

8. **Ask a trend question:**
   - Type: **"Is our overall denial rate trending up or down over the last 6 months?"**
   - "If it's trending up, I need to escalate. If it's flat, my interventions are holding. If it's down, my team is making progress."

### Closing

This platform gives me denial intelligence with AI-powered anomaly detection, cost visibility by facility and department, and conversational analytics that let me ask follow-up questions without waiting for a report refresh. And the governance model ensures financial data stays in financial hands."

## Appendix: CMS Program Alignment

The following metrics deployed in Provider 360 directly relate to CMS programs. Use this reference during demos when speaking to compliance, quality, or executive audiences.

### Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP)

CMS penalizes hospitals up to 3% of base DRG payments for excess 30-day readmissions.

| Metric | Definition | Semantic View |
|--------|-----------|:---:|
| READMISSION_RATE | % of encounters with a 30-day readmit | Yes |
| READMIT_30_FLAG | Boolean flag per encounter | Yes (via mart) |
| AVG_LOS_INDEX | Actual LOS / DRG-expected LOS | Yes |
| LONG_STAY_PCT | % of stays exceeding expected LOS | Yes |
| LOS_VARIANCE | Actual minus expected LOS in days | Mart only |

**Applicable DRGs in demo data:** AMI (280-282), Heart Failure (291-293), Pneumonia (193-195), COPD (190-192), Hip/Knee (469-470), CABG (233-234)

---

### Inpatient Prospective Payment System (IPPS / MS-DRG)

CMS pays hospitals a fixed amount per discharge based on MS-DRG classification and weight.

| Metric | Definition | Semantic View |
|--------|-----------|:---:|
| DRG_WEIGHT | CMS MS-DRG case-mix weight | Yes |
| GEOMETRIC_LOS | CMS benchmark LOS per DRG | Yes (via dimension) |
| LOS_INDEX | Actual / Expected LOS ratio | Yes |
| COST_PER_CMI_DISCHARGE | Cost normalized by case-mix index | No (mart only) |
| CMI_ADJUSTED_DISCHARGES | Discharges weighted by avg DRG weight | No (mart only) |

---

### CMS-HCC Risk Adjustment Model

Used for Medicare Advantage capitation payments. Higher HCC scores = higher expected cost = higher payment.

| Metric | Definition | Semantic View |
|--------|-----------|:---:|
| HCC_SCORE | Patient-level CMS-HCC risk score | Yes |
| HCC_FLAG | Whether a diagnosis maps to an HCC category | No (dimension) |
| HCC_CATEGORY | Specific HCC category (e.g., HCC18, HCC85) | No (dimension) |

**27 HCC categories mapped** across ~100 ICD-10 codes in the demo data.

---

### Medicare Star Ratings / HEDIS / MIPS Quality

CMS Star Ratings drive Medicare Advantage bonus payments. MIPS adjusts physician fee schedule payments.

| Metric | Measure | CMS Use | Semantic View |
|--------|---------|---------|:---:|
| CBP | Controlling Blood Pressure | Star C09, MIPS Quality | No (fact only) |
| A1C | HbA1c Control for Diabetes | Star C11, MIPS Quality | No (fact only) |
| BCS | Breast Cancer Screening | Star C01, MIPS Quality | No (fact only) |
| COL | Colorectal Cancer Screening | Star C02, MIPS Quality | No (fact only) |
| SPC | Statin Therapy for CVD | Star C16, MIPS Quality | No (fact only) |

**Note:** These exist in FCT_CARE_GAP but are not yet exposed in the semantic view or marts.

---

### Value-Based Purchasing (VBP)

CMS adjusts hospital DRG payments +/- 2% across four domains.

| VBP Domain | Provider 360 Metric | Semantic View |
|-----------|-------------------|:---:|
| Clinical Outcomes | READMISSION_RATE | Yes |
| Efficiency & Cost Reduction | AVG_LOS_INDEX, COST_PER_CMI_DISCHARGE | Partial |
| Safety | (not modeled — HAC metrics) | — |
| Person & Community Engagement | (not modeled — HCAHPS) | — |

---

### Medicare Claims & Revenue Cycle

| Metric | Definition | Semantic View |
|--------|-----------|:---:|
| TOTAL_DENIED_CHARGES | Sum of denied claim line charges | Yes |
| APPEAL_RATE | % of denied lines that were appealed | Yes |
| TIMELY_FILING_PCT | % of claims filed within payer deadline | Yes |
| DENIAL_CATEGORY | Eligibility, Prior Auth, Coding, Medical Necessity | Yes (via mart) |

**CMS relevance:** Medicare claims follow CMS billing rules; denial categories directly relate to CMS documentation requirements and coding accuracy.

## Demo Tips (All Personas)

- **Role switching** is the single most impactful governance demo. Toggle between roles on the Patient Records page to show dynamic masking in action.
- **The Chat Widget** is available on every page. Use it opportunistically when a question arises naturally during any persona's walkthrough.
- **ML features** (forecast, anomaly, top insights) require no external tools — emphasize "no Python, no notebooks, no SageMaker" when discussing with technical audiences.
- **Data freshness**: All data is synthetic (Jul 2023 - Dec 2024). If asked, emphasize this is realistic healthcare data patterns with synthetic PHI.
- **Snowflake positioning**: Each persona's script naturally highlights different Snowflake capabilities — Cortex ML for the Analyst, dynamic masking for the Clinician, Cortex Agent for the Executive, and anomaly detection for Finance. This lets you tailor the "why Snowflake" message to the audience.
