# Week 2 Plan: Conformed Data Model + Synthetic Data

## Context

**Current state:** 5 databases, 27 schemas, 4 warehouses, 6 roles, governance objects all in place. No tables exist yet (except 2 governance mapping tables). RAW is empty.

**Scale:** Demo-scale (10%) — ~25K patients, ~200K encounters, ~1.2M claims lines, 18 months of history (Jul 2023 – Dec 2024).

**Approach:** Generate all synthetic data directly in Snowflake using SQL `GENERATOR()` and `UNIFORM()`/`RANDOM()` functions — no external Python generator needed. This keeps everything self-contained, avoids file staging, and runs fast on `H360_LOAD_WH`.

### Step 4: Populate dimensions with realistic synthetic data
In `infrastructure/08_seed_dimensions.sql`:
- `DIM_DATE`: Generated from date spine
- `DIM_FACILITY`: 5 facilities (1 main hospital, 2 community, 1 rehab, 1 ambulatory surgery center)
- `DIM_DEPARTMENT`: ~30 departments across facilities (ED, ICU, Med-Surg, OR, L&D, Cardiology, Ortho, etc.)
- `DIM_PAYER`: ~15 payers (Medicare, Medicaid, BCBS, UHC, Aetna, Cigna, Humana, self-pay, etc.) with 80/20 mix
- `DIM_DIAGNOSIS_ICD10`: ~200 common ICD-10 codes (top diagnoses by volume)
- `DIM_PROCEDURE_CPT`: ~150 common CPT codes with RVU values
- `DIM_DRG`: ~80 common MS-DRGs with weights and LOS benchmarks
- `DIM_MEDICATION_RXNORM`: ~100 common medications with drug class and unit costs
- `DIM_PATIENT`: 25,000 patients with realistic demographics, ZIP distribution, payer assignment
- `DIM_PROVIDER`: ~200 providers across specialties

### Step 5: Populate fact tables with synthetic data
In `infrastructure/09_seed_facts.sql`:
- `FCT_ENCOUNTER`: ~200K encounters (18 months) with seasonal patterns, LOS distributions by DRG, realistic charge/cost/payment amounts
- `FCT_ADT_EVENT`: ~600K events (admit/transfer/discharge per encounter)
- `FCT_OR_CASE`: ~30K OR cases with block utilization patterns
- `FCT_CLAIM_LINE`: ~1.2M claim lines with 80/20 payer mix, 8-12% denial rate
- `FCT_REMITTANCE`: ~1.1M remittance lines (matched to claims)
- `FCT_REFERRAL`: ~50K referrals with ~15% leakage rate
- `FCT_CARE_GAP`: ~75K care gap records (5 HEDIS measures × 25K patients, ~60% open)
- `FCT_EHR_USAGE`: ~200 providers × 365 days = ~73K records
- `FCT_LABOR_HOUR`: ~2K employees × 365 days = ~730K records
- `FCT_GL_TRANSACTION`: ~500K GL lines
- `FCT_SUPPLY_USAGE`: ~400K supply records
- `FCT_BED_STATUS`: ~300K status changes

### Step 6: Apply governance (tags + masking policies to columns)
In `infrastructure/10_apply_governance.sql`:
- Tag PHI columns (SSN, DOB, MRN, names) with `DATA_CLASSIFICATION = 'PHI'`
- Tag PII columns (phone, email, address) with `DATA_CLASSIFICATION = 'PII'`
- Attach masking policies to tagged columns
- Populate RAP mapping tables with demo dept/facility assignments per role
