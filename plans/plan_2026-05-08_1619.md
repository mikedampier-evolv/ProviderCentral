## Context

`FCT_GL_TRANSACTION` has 500K rows with categories: LABOR, SUPPLIES, OVERHEAD, DEPRECIATION (all debits/expenses) and REVENUE (credits). Data spans July 2023 – Dec 2024 across all 30 departments and 5 facilities. This joins naturally to encounter volume by department + month for cost-per-discharge and margin analysis.

## Use Case: Financial Performance (UC6)

**Question answered**: "What is our cost structure by department and facility, and where are the margin opportunities?"

**Mart grain**: 1 row per department per fiscal period (month) — aggregates GL expenses/revenue and clinical volume.
